Civil Time vs. Scientific time

When mechanical clocks began to be used to show hours of daylight or nighttime,
their period needed to be changed every morning and evening (for example, by
changing the length of their pendula). The use of 24 hours for the entire day
meant hours varied much less and the clocks needed to be adjusted only a few
times a month.
